
# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales data to uncover key business insights, trends, and performance metrics. The goal is to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions by identifying top-performing products, customer behavior patterns, and revenue trends.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing products and brands
- Understand customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Track sales trends over time
- Highlight key business insights for decision-making

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description
The dataset contains transactional sales records, including:
- Order Date & Ship Date  
- Product Category & Brand  
- Sales & Profit  
- Customer Information (demographics, location)  
- Order and shipping details  

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
The dataset was processed to ensure accuracy and consistency:
- Converted date columns to proper date format  
- Handled missing values and removed duplicates  
- Standardized categorical data (e.g., product names, regions)  
- Validated key fields such as sales and profit  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- **Sales Performance Overview** – Total revenue, profit, and KPIs  
- **Sales Trends** – Time-based analysis of sales growth  
- **Product & Brand Insights** – Top-performing and low-performing products  
- **Customer Analysis** – Spending patterns and demographics  
- **Regional Insights** – Performance by location  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboard)  
- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Certain products consistently drive the highest revenue  
- Seasonal trends impact sales performance  
- A small percentage of customers contribute significantly to total sales  
- Some regions outperform others in profitability  

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Open the Power BI dashboard file  
2. Use filters and slicers to explore different segments  
3. Interact with charts to gain deeper insights
